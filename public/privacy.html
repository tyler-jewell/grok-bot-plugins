Privacy note

SkiHerd is hosted free by Tyler Jewell as a bare-minimum public demo. It is not a commercial product yet.

  • We do not ask for an email, phone number, or Cloudflare account.
  • A group stores only display names, unguessable tokens (as hashes), and the location points members post.
  • Every group, and all of its data, is deleted automatically 24 hours after the group is created. No data is kept after that deletion.
  • Do not post a child’s location, or anyone’s location, without their informed consent. This service is not directed at children under 13.
  • Cloudflare, as the infrastructure provider, may retain operational request logs according to its own policies, even after group storage is deleted. Those logs are not used by SkiHerd as a product database.
  • Map tiles and the Winter Park trail list come from OpenStreetMap. This project is not affiliated with Winter Park Resort.

Questions: open an issue on the public repository. See also the terms.
