Terms of use

SkiHerd is a free, experimental group map hosted by Tyler Jewell. It is provided as-is, without warranty, at a bare minimum of features and reliability.

  • You may create a group, invite up to nine other people, and post locations for up to 24 hours.
  • Everything in a group is deleted 24 hours after creation. Do not rely on SkiHerd as a record of a ski day.
  • Do not use the service to track anyone without their consent. Do not use it to locate children.
